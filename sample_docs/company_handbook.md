# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Requests for full-time
remote work must be approved by a department director. All remote employees
must be available on Slack between 10am and 4pm UK time.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year, increasing to
30 days after 5 years of continuous service. Leave requests must be submitted
at least 2 weeks in advance via the HR portal.

## Expense Reimbursement

Employees can claim reimbursement for business expenses up to £500 without
prior manager approval. Expenses above this threshold require written
sign-off from a line manager before being incurred. All claims must be
submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can be taken
within the first 12 months following the birth or adoption of a child.
