# Aldermere Advisory — Standard Rate Card

*Synthetic commercial reference data for the proposal-response demo corpus.
Effective for proposals issued in the current financial year.*

## Day rates by grade

| Grade | Day rate (excl. VAT) |
|---|---|
| Managing Partner | £1,850 |
| Engagement Director | £1,450 |
| Senior Consultant | £1,050 |
| Consultant | £750 |
| Business Analyst | £550 |

A typical embedded team (one Engagement Director, one Senior Consultant, one
Business Analyst) blends to approximately £950 per day across the team.

## Commercial terms

- **Payment terms:** net 30 days from invoice date. Invoices are issued
  monthly in arrears unless the proposal specifies fixed-price milestone
  billing.
- **Expenses:** charged at cost, capped at 12% of professional fees unless a
  higher cap is agreed in writing before the engagement starts.
- **Rate validity:** day rates hold for 12 months from the date of the
  proposal, after which they are subject to review in line with RPI.
- **Minimum engagement length:** four weeks, except for fixed-scope
  diagnostic engagements priced separately.
- **Notice period:** either party may pause or terminate an ongoing
  engagement on four weeks' written notice, without prejudice to fees
  already accrued.
- **Fixed-price option:** available for engagements with a clearly defined
  scope and deliverable set, priced at proposal stage from the day-rate
  card above; fixed-price engagements are not subject to the notice-period
  clause above, and are instead governed by the milestone schedule in the
  specific proposal.

## What this rate card does not cover

This rate card applies to standard advisory and delivery engagements only.
It does not include: expert witness or litigation support work, which is
quoted separately; engagements requiring on-site presence outside the UK,
which carry a location surcharge agreed case by case; or subcontracted
specialist skills outside Aldermere's four practice areas, which are passed
through at the subcontractor's cost plus an agreed margin, disclosed in the
relevant proposal.
