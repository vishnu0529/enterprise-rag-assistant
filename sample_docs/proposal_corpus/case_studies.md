# Aldermere Advisory — Case Studies

*Synthetic case studies for the proposal-response demo corpus. Client names are
withheld and described only by sector and size, matching how Aldermere
actually anonymises past work in real proposals.*

## Case study 1 — Finance function redesign, regional housing association

**Client:** a regional housing association managing approximately 14,000
properties, engaged 2023.

**Situation:** month-end close routinely took 12 working days, driven by
manual reconciliation between the repairs and rent systems and a finance team
that relied on agency temps for 40% of transactional processing capacity
during peak periods.

**Approach:** a 14-week engagement across Discover (2 weeks), Design
(3 weeks), Deliver (7 weeks) and Embed (2 weeks) phases, led by Managing
Partner Priya Narayanan as engagement sponsor with day-to-day delivery run by
a two-person embedded team.

**Result:** month-end close reduced from 12 working days to 5 working days.
Reliance on agency staff for transactional finance work fell by 30% within
two reporting cycles of go-live. The client renewed for a follow-on
systems-migration engagement the following year.

## Case study 2 — Digital operations transformation, mid-market retailer

**Client:** a UK homeware retailer with 34 stores and a growing online
channel, engaged 2024.

**Situation:** the order-to-fulfilment cycle averaged 6.2 days against a
sector benchmark closer to 4 days, and the finance team spent an estimated
480 hours per quarter on manual reconciliation between the e-commerce
platform and the warehouse management system.

**Approach:** a 16-week engagement led by Engagement Director Tom Whitfield,
covering process mapping, a targeted workflow-automation build, and a
phased store-by-store rollout to limit disruption during trading hours.

**Result:** order-to-fulfilment cycle time reduced by 35%, bringing it below
the sector benchmark. Manual reconciliation effort fell from 480 to
approximately 90 hours per quarter. No stores reported a fulfilment
disruption during rollout.

## Case study 3 — AI-augmented document review pilot, regional law firm

**Client:** a regional law firm with four offices, engaged 2025.

**Situation:** first-draft turnaround on standard engagement letters and
routine client correspondence averaged 3 working days, gated on associate
availability rather than partner review time.

**Approach:** a 10-week pilot led by Dr Ines Falk, applying a
retrieval-augmented drafting tool over the firm's own template library and
past correspondence, with mandatory partner sign-off retained on every
document before it left the firm — the pilot changed drafting speed, not the
firm's approval chain.

**Result:** first-draft turnaround fell from 3 working days to approximately
4 hours. Partner sign-off was required, and obtained, on 100% of documents
produced during the pilot; no document reached a client without human review.
The firm extended the pilot to two further document categories after the
initial 10 weeks.
