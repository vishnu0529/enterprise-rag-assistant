# Aldermere Advisory — Standard Commercial Terms

*Synthetic standard terms for the proposal-response demo corpus. A real
proposal would attach the full signed terms; this is the summary Aldermere's
bid team draws on when answering RFP questions about commercial terms.*

## Confidentiality

Aldermere and the client enter a mutual non-disclosure agreement at
proposal stage, ahead of any detailed discovery work. Confidentiality
obligations survive termination of the engagement for 3 years.

## Intellectual property

The client owns the intellectual property in deliverables produced
specifically for them under the engagement — reports, process designs,
system configurations, and similar. Aldermere retains ownership of its own
pre-existing methodologies, frameworks, and reusable tooling (including the
retrieval-augmented drafting approach used in Applied AI & Automation
engagements), and grants the client a perpetual licence to use the outputs
produced from them for that client's own internal purposes.

## Liability

Aldermere's liability under an engagement is capped at 100% of the fees paid
by the client in the 12 months preceding the claim, except for claims
arising from gross negligence, wilful misconduct, or breach of
confidentiality, which are uncapped. Aldermere holds professional indemnity
insurance of £5 million per claim, and public liability insurance of
£10 million.

## Data handling

All client data is processed and stored within the UK/EEA. Aldermere
processes personal data as a data processor under UK GDPR, under
instructions set out in a Data Processing Agreement signed alongside the
main engagement contract. On written request at the end of an engagement,
Aldermere deletes or returns all client data within 30 days, and provides
written confirmation of deletion where requested.

## Escalation and dispute resolution

Day-to-day issues are escalated first to the named engagement lead, then to
the Managing Partner if unresolved within 5 working days. Unresolved
commercial disputes are subject to mediation before either party may pursue
formal legal proceedings, under the engagement's governing contract.

## What these terms do not cover

These standard terms apply to advisory and delivery engagements under
Aldermere's standard rate card. They do not extend to bespoke contractual
arrangements — indemnities beyond the standard cap, non-standard IP
assignment, or non-UK data residency — which must be agreed in writing on a
case-by-case basis and are not covered by anything in this corpus.
