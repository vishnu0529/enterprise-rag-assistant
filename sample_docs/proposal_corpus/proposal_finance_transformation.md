## Understanding of the requirement

The client's finance team was closing the books in 12 working days, well
outside the 5 to 7 day range typical for a housing association of its size,
driven by manual reconciliation between the repairs system and the general
ledger, and by dependence on agency temps for 40% of transactional
processing capacity at peak.

## Proposed approach

We proposed a 14-week engagement in four phases:

- **Discover (2 weeks):** process mapping of the current close cycle,
  root-cause analysis of the reconciliation gap, baseline measurement.
- **Design (3 weeks):** target operating model for the finance function,
  automation design for the repairs-to-ledger reconciliation, staffing plan
  to reduce agency dependence.
- **Deliver (7 weeks):** build and test the automated reconciliation,
  phased staff transition, two parallel close cycles run under the new
  process before full cutover.
- **Embed (2 weeks):** handover to the client's own finance team, training,
  and a 30-day hypercare period.

## Relevant experience

This proposal drew directly on Aldermere's Finance Function Redesign
practice track record; the resulting engagement is now documented as Case
Study 1, having reduced month-end close from 12 working days to 5 and cut
agency-staff dependence by 30% within two reporting cycles of go-live.

## Risk and mitigation

The principal delivery risk identified was parallel-running two close
cycles without disrupting statutory reporting deadlines; this was mitigated
by scheduling the parallel-run period outside the client's year-end close
window and keeping the legacy process live as a fallback until the new
process had completed two clean cycles.
