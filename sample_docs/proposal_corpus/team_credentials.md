## Standard staffing note for proposals

Aldermere staffs engagements with a minimum of one Engagement Director or
above as engagement lead, plus at least one Senior Consultant, for any
engagement exceeding four weeks. Business Analyst support is added where an
engagement has a significant data-preparation or measurement component. CVs
for named individuals are confirmed at proposal stage and may be substituted
only with the client's written agreement.
