## Who we are

Aldermere Advisory LLP is a UK management and technology consultancy founded in
2014, headquartered in London with a second office in Manchester. We employ 140
staff across four practice areas and work exclusively with organisations of
50 to 500 employees — large enough to run a real transformation programme,
small enough that they cannot justify a permanent in-house delivery team for it.

## Practice areas

- **Operations & Process Transformation** — process redesign, workflow
  automation, and operating-model change for back-office and service functions.
- **Finance Function Redesign** — month-end close acceleration, finance systems
  migration, and finance-team operating model design.
- **Applied AI & Automation** — retrieval-augmented and agentic tooling for
  document-heavy workflows, delivered with the same evaluation and
  governance discipline as our non-AI engagements.
- **Programme & Change Management** — programme assurance, stakeholder
  management, and change adoption for multi-workstream transformations.

## Sectors served

Professional services firms, financial services, housing associations and
wider public sector, and mid-market retail. We do not work with organisations
above 500 employees; our delivery model and pricing are built around
firms of that size, not enterprise accounts.

## Track record

- 47 engagements delivered since 2019.
- Average client Net Promoter Score of 62 across post-engagement surveys.
- 84% of new engagements originate from repeat clients or direct referral,
  not competitive tender.
- Zero engagements terminated early for cause since the firm's founding.

## Accreditations and standards

- ISO 27001:2022 certified (information security management).
- Cyber Essentials Plus certified, renewed annually.
- Full member of the Management Consultancies Association (MCA).
- All client data processed and stored within the UK/EEA; see our standard
  terms for the full data-handling commitment.

## What makes our delivery different

We embed a small team inside the client's own operating rhythm rather than
running parallel workstreams that hand over at the end. Every engagement
carries a named engagement lead who is personally accountable for outcomes,
not just a delivery manager rotated in from the bench. Where an engagement
uses AI-assisted tooling, we hold ourselves to the same standard we would ask
a client's own IT function to meet: a defined acceptance threshold, human
sign-off on every output that reaches a client or regulator, and a documented
rollback path if the tooling underperforms in production.
